## Science and references

Species information was checked against conservative, published sources and kept to well-established facts:

- IUCN Red List of Threatened Species (status wording per species; "No assessment found" where none exists)
- World Register of Marine Species (WoRMS) and FishBase for names, sizes and depth ranges
- NOAA Ocean Exploration and Schmidt Ocean Institute expedition reports for deep habitats of the Mariana region
- Lupton et al. (2006), *Submarine venting of liquid carbon dioxide on a Mariana Arc volcano*, G³ — the "Champagne"
  vent of NW Eifuku, the model for the liquid-CO₂ seep
- Chiba et al. (2018), *Human footprint in the abyss: 30 year records of deep-sea plastic debris*, Marine Policy —
  a plastic bag recorded at 10,898 m in the Mariana Trench
- Minderoo–UWA Deep Sea Research Centre and Tokyo University of Marine Science and Technology (2023) — a snailfish
  filmed at about 8,336 m in the Izu–Ogasawara Trench, the deepest fish yet recorded
- Bühlmann ZHL-16C decompression model (Bühlmann & Völlm, *Tauchmedizin*) for the wrist computer
- U.S. Navy Diving Manual — surface decompression with oxygen, used for the chamber sequence

Simplifications for play: depth, time and stops are compressed; the decompression shown is not dive guidance.
